Appendix · Trust by Design

Useful evidence without automated judgment

The system identifies where to look—people decide what it means.

Evidence boundary

Disagreement is a signal

Two transcripts disagreeing creates a review candidate, not an automatic pronunciation verdict.

Privacy boundary

Audio stays bounded
