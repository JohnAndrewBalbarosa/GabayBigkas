The Solution

Prompt → Speak → Compare → Guide → Retry

An AI coach built around the learner’s actual voice.

AI coach

Prompts a reading or conversation aligned with DepEd, CHED, or another trusted learning institution.

Live voice

Agora carries the conversation while the session audio and transcript are captured.

Second listen

BuzzASR produces a second timestamped transcript designed for Filipino and Tagalog speech contexts.

Deterministic diff

The system parses both transcripts and identifies the exact words on which they disagree.

Guided retry

A disagreement triggers trusted pronunciation guidance; the learner retries until both transcripts agree.

Be precise about the confusion matrix. This weekly audit samples agreement cases, which are negative predictions.
