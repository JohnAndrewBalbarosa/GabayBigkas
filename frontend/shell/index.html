OUR MISSION

About GabayBigkas

GabayBigkas is a local-first English learning lab built to empower non-native English speakers. Traditional automated pronunciation tools often give ambiguous percentage scores without actionable diagnostic guidance.

Our philosophy is evidence-first: transcript disagreements are treated as review signals rather than harsh verdicts. Learners get human-level review insights, verified phoneme feedback, and focused multimedia practice materials.

Architecture Highlights

  • Local-First & Privacy-Preserving: Audio recordings are strictly duration-bounded and processed only with adult consent.
  • Zero Cloud Audio Retention: Temporary session chunks are securely purged post-analysis.
  • Standard Axum & Rust Engine: High-performance audio preprocessing and idempotent event synchronization.
