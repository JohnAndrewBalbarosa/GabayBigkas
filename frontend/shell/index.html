The Challenge

Every learner needs feedback, but teachers in large classrooms cannot hear every attempt. When feedback is delayed, hesitation speaks first and learners miss chances to practice.

02

Beyond Generic Transcripts

Ordinary speech recognition merely types out what it heard without turning uncertainty into guidance. GabayBigkas treats speech differences as diagnostic signals, not failure.

03

Every Voice Deserves A Clearer Next Try

By pairing live Agora voice streaming with fine-tuned acoustic comparison, learners receive targeted guidance and retry immediately until both engines agree.

The 5-Step Learning Loop

1. Prompt Curated sentence prompt aligned with learning benchmarks
2. Speak Live voice carried via Agora WebRTC with high-fidelity capture
3. Second Listen Acoustic model tuned for Philippine English speech contexts
4. Deterministic Diff Pinpoints exact words and phonemes with transcription differences
5. Guided Retry Targeted pronunciation tips and video lessons for instant re-attempt

“Disagreement drives the next practice step. Agreement is what we audit.”

— GabayBigkas Design Philosophy

System Trust & Safety

  • Zero Cloud Audio Retention: Audio recordings are strictly duration-bounded and processed only with adult consent. Temporary session audio is cleaned up immediately post-analysis.
  • Traceable Human Review: Reviewers and educators audit agreement and edge cases through a dedicated queue.
  • Native High-Performance Core: Rust Axum backend with idempotent event handling and server-held proxy security.
